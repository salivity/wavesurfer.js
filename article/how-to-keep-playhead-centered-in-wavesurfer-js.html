How to Keep Playhead Centered in Wavesurfer.js

This article explains how to fix the playhead at the center of the viewport while the audio waveform scrolls dynamically underneath during playback in Wavesurfer.js. By setting specific configuration options and ensuring appropriate zoom levels, developers can easily achieve a modern, centered-scrub audio player interface.

The primary option used to keep the playhead centered in Wavesurfer.js is autoCenter.

When initializing Wavesurfer, setting autoCenter: true tells the player to automatically scroll the container so that the current playback position remains in the center of the view.

const wavesurfer = WaveSurfer.create({
  container: '#waveform',
  waveColor: '#4F4A85',
  progressColor: '#383351',
  url: '/path/to/audio.mp3',
  autoCenter: true,
  minPxPerSec: 100, // Necessary to enable horizontal scrolling
});

Key Requirements for the Centered Effect

  1. Enable Zooming / Scrolling: The centered playhead effect requires the waveform to be wider than the wrapper container. Use the minPxPerSec parameter during initialization or call the wavesurfer.zoom(pxPerSec) method dynamically to expand the waveform. If the entire audio fits inside the visible container, scrolling cannot occur.

  2. autoCenter Behavior: In Wavesurfer.js (version 7 and newer), autoCenter defaults to true. When the track plays or when a user seeks, the container smoothly shifts the waveform underneath the playhead once the cursor reaches the midpoint of the visible track.

  3. Smooth Playback: For continuous and fluid scrolling, ensure that minPxPerSec is set to a sufficiently high value (typically 50–100 or higher depending on the audio length), allowing the rendering engine to scroll smoothly with the playback frame rate.
